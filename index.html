Speech LLM for ASR

Achieved less than 10% Word Error Rate (WER) using self-supervised models like HuBERT and LLMs to perform multi-modal multi-tasking with discrete speech units for Automatic Speech Recognition. Performed continual pretraining and fine-tuning of Large Language Models using QLoRA within FSDP settings, processing ∼1 TB of speech and text data.

Enables the model to handle out-of-vocabulary keywords and unseen languages. Trained on 38K keywords from the English split of the MLCommons Multilingual Spoken Words Corpus, achieving 0.8 MTWV.
